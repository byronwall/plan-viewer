# Ship workspace invites

Owners can invite teammates by email. Each invite is a single-use link that expires after seven days, and owners can see and revoke pending invites. Tracked as ATL-412, with the empty-workspace entry point in OPS-111; the design lives in [plans/invites](../plans/invites/README.md).

## Outcome

A new teammate can go from an invite email to a working account in under a minute, and nobody can join a workspace from a stale or forwarded link.
